How it works

  1. Free 15-minute consult. You tell me what's going on. I tell you honestly whether I'm the right fit.
  2. Gather the picture. Income, spending, debts, accounts, and what's happening in your life. Everything you share stays confidential.
  3. Build the plan. Goals with numbers and dates, a spending plan you'll use, and the first three moves.
  4. Work it together. Weekly sessions in the six-week package, or a single session when you need a reset.

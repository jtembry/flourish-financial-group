What you can expect to walk away with

Counseling sessions

  • A written spending plan built on your real numbers, by the end of session two.
  • A debt payoff plan with an order, a monthly amount, and a projected payoff date.
  • Your credit reports reviewed line by line, with disputes filed for anything wrong.
  • An emergency-fund target sized to your life, and the account it lives in opened.
  • Tax withholding checked so you stop lending the IRS money, or owing in April.
  • A one-page financial snapshot you can hand to a spouse, lawyer, or lender.

Life transitions

  • A move budget that covers the gap between last paycheck and first.
  • A "what can we actually afford" number for a home, including taxes, insurance, and upkeep, before you talk to a lender.
  • A pre-approval checklist and a credit plan timed to your purchase date.

Military families

  • A budget that survives PCS, deployment, and the pay changes that come with them.
  • Military benefits and SCRA checklist reviewed for anything you're leaving on the table.
  • A transition-to-civilian budget built on the pay you'll actually take home.

Young adults

  • Your first budget, first credit card used correctly, and your workplace retirement match captured.
  • A student loan repayment plan chosen on purpose, not by default.

Booking, payment, and rescheduling

Sessions are by Google Meet, weekdays 4:00–5:30 pm et · weekend afternoons. Payment is taken when you book. Reschedule free up to 24 hours before; inside 24 hours the session is forfeited except for emergencies, which we'll work out like humans. Packages are non-refundable after the first session; unused sessions stay good for 12 months.
